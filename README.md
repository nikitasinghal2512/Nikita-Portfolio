## 🔬 Visual Identity & Creative Architecture

1. **Custom 3D Animated DNA Structure & Molecular Particles**:
   - Replaced the previous molecular constellation with a custom, continuous 3D animated DNA double helix.
   - Interactive pointer parallax, spherical nucleotide nodes with depth sorting, 3D specular highlights, and floating solvent micro-particles.
   - **Dark Mode**: Electric Blue (`#168CFF`) helical strands, Ice Cyan (`#49E6FF`) secondary strands and solvent ions, with selective **Neon Coral-Red (`#FF527A`)** regulatory bases.
   - **Light Mode**: Deep Aubergine (`#3A0F34`) helical strands, soft silver rungs, and selective **Marigold (`#EAA000`)** active bases.
   - Uncluttered composition with smooth rotation respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`.

2. **Distinctive Name Calligraphy & Editorial Typography**:
   - Expressive editorial headline: *“Curiosity for life. Designed by science.”* set in *Cormorant Garamond* with an italicized second line.
   - **Nikita Singhal** styled in the flowing, natural calligraphic script *Pinyon Script* (`.font-script`) with a gentle opacity entrance animation.
   - Interface text set in clean, modern *Manrope*.

3. **Refined Navigation (No CV Button)**:
   - Wordmark at top-left (*Nikita Singhal • BIOTECHNOLOGY*) serves as the Home view control.
   - Central tabs: **About | Interests | Education | Projects | Skills | Contact** with a slim active underline indicator.
   - Completely removed the Curriculum Vitae button from the header, maintaining balanced spacing.

4. **Exact Dual-Theme System**:
   - **Dark Mode**: Layered midnight-black and deep-navy (`#080C16`), secondary surfaces (`#101827`, `#151F31`), electric blue (`#168CFF`), ice cyan (`#49E6FF`), and neon coral-red (`#FF527A`).
   - **Light Mode**: Ice Silver (`#DDE3E8`), Deep Aubergine (`#3A0F34`), Marigold (`#EAA000`), and supporting Muted Marsh Brown (`#765A55`).
